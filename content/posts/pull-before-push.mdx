Slovníček stores its vocabulary in IndexedDB and treats it as the source of
truth, not as a cache. That one decision creates the hardest problem in the
whole project: when the same row has been edited in two places, which edit wins?

## The naive version, and why it loses data

server row.

## Ordering is the fix, not conflict resolution

Explain why pulling first is what makes last-write-wins safe, rather than the
comparison itself. The comparison was never the hard part.
